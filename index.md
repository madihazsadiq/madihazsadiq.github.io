<p>
Hello! I am a PhD researcher in the Department of Political and Social Sciences at the European University Institute (Florence, Italy).
</p>

<p>
My thesis examines Japanese postwar repatriates from Manchuria, focusing on how they experienced a shift in status due to regime change, developments in local postwar economies, and wartime violence. I conduct archival research, digitise historical data, and use a combination of regression analysis and qualitative content analysis to trace this. I am extending the project to investigate how repatriates have shaped collective memory of war in contemporary Japan. Broadly, my work sits at the intersection of historical political economy and sociology.
</p>

<p>
In Autumn 2024, I was a visiting scholar at Waseda University (Tokyo, Japan). At the EUI, I co-organised the Political Behaviour Colloquium (2023/2024). Since 2020, I have worked as a research assistant for multiple projects at the University of Birmingham and the University of Leeds (UK), and prior to this, I was an INGO researcher working on development-focused research projects across the Global South.
</p>

<p>
My general research interests are in political culture, migration, group identities and relations, networks, diffusion, and conflict.
</p>
